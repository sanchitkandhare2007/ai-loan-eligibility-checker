Underwriting Workflow

How the 100-Point Scoring Engine Works

The application evaluates seven core inputs through five mathematical stages:

Metric Name Weight Formula / Logic Viva Explanation
FOIR (Debt-to-Income) 35 Points (Existing EMI + New EMI) / Income × 100 Measures whether the borrower has sufficient disposable income left after debt payments.
Credit Score (CIBIL/FICO) 30 Points Tiered scale (300 to 900) Quantifies past repayment discipline; scores >750 receive full points.
Loan-to-Annual-Income (LTI) 15 Points Loan Amount / (Monthly Income × 12) Guards against over-leveraging by checking loan size relative to annual salary.
Employment Stability 10 Points Experience Years + Employment Type Rewards longer career tenure and regular salaried employment.
Age Bracket 10 Points Prime earning curve (24 - 52 yrs) Ensures applicant has an active working horizon to service multi-year debt.
